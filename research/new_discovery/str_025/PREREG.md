# STR-025: volatility-scaled sizing using IND-018 (pre-registered 2026-11-13, before computation)

**Idea.** IND-018, the HAR volatility forecast, was the only INFORMATIVE indicator: it predicts next-day volatility in all 5 markets. It says nothing about direction. So it is used here to set **position size**: smaller when high volatility is forecast, larger when low volatility is forecast. The question is whether that cuts drawdowns without lowering risk-adjusted return.

## Test on historical trades (STR-023, the only strategy currently on WATCH with a long history)

- **Base trades:** STR-023, VWAP momentum BUY with the trailing exit. They are generated by the frozen `rsi_vwap_study` code on its frozen data: BTC (`btc_breakout/data`, other branch, read only) and ETH (study data), 2020-01 → 2026-11, R at the primary cost of 0.05%.
- **Forecast** (causal):
  - daily realised variance from 1-hour closes (UTC days);
  - HAR (1, 5, 22 days) fitted by OLS on an expanding window, refitted at each month start;
  - the first forecast after 150 days of data;
  - the forecast for a trade's entry day uses only completed days before it.
- **Weight:** *w* = clip(σ\* / σ̂, 0.25, 2), where σ̂ = √forecast and σ\* = the median of σ̂ over the previous 365 days (causal).
- **Comparison at equal average exposure:**
  - sized R = *w* / mean(*w*) × R, with mean(*w*) taken over the same period's trades;
  - against unsized R;
  - metrics: mean R, mean / sd per trade, max drawdown of cumulative R, and the worst 20-trade run.
- **Samples:** BTC and ETH, each in two halves (2020-01 → 2023-06 and 2023-07 → 2026-11). That gives 4 comparisons.

## Verdict

| Verdict | Conditions |
|---|---|
| **USEFUL** | In all 4 comparisons, max drawdown lower **and** mean / sd not lower by more than 5% |
| **MIXED** | 2–3 of the 4 |
| **NOT USEFUL** | 0–1 |

## Forward

If the overlay is USEFUL or MIXED, it is reported as a **read-only shadow** on the forward ledgers of STR-023 (and STR-008). The forward tests themselves are not changed.
